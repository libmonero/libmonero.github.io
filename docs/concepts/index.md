# Concepts

The ideas behind the libraries, in plain words, for who is new to Monero or to these libraries. They explain **why** the examples do what they do, and each page points to the examples that use it.

* [How the pieces fit](how-it-fits.md): the node, the wallet server, the native wallet and your application.
* [Units and networks](units-and-networks.md): atomic units, and the four networks that a program can run on.
* [Accounts and subaddresses](accounts-and-subaddresses.md): how one wallet gives out many addresses.
* [Seeds, keys and restore height](seeds-keys-and-restore-height.md): what a wallet is made of, and what to back up.
* [Confirmations and locks](confirmations-and-locks.md): when a payment is final, and when funds can be spent.

For the code, see the [examples](../examples/index.md).
